Product & Fabric Questions

All Paithani sarees from Bansilal Textile come with a Silk Mark India certification tag and a handloom certificate verifying origin from Yeola or Paithan weaving centres. The zari threads undergo a burn test and spectrometer verification. You can also look for the characteristic "ekdhoti" weaving on the reverse where the pallu meets the body.
Dry clean only for Paithani, Kanjivaram, and Banarasi silks. Store folded in soft muslin cloth, never in plastic. Air your sarees after wearing before folding to prevent mildew. Avoid spraying perfume directly on silk. For zari sarees, periodically spread them to prevent permanent fold marks. Never wring or machine wash.
