## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal grandeur of Indian artisanal textiles paired with the poise of contemporary haute couture. The personality is opulent yet quiet, rooted in generations of weaving mastery while speaking directly to discerning global collectors of luxury ethnic wear, bespoke bridal couture, and heirloom weaves.

The visual direction merges **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Tactile Heritage Metaphors**. Rather than overt ornamentation, the interface prioritizes expansive breathing room, immaculate typography, and micro-textures that recall raw tussar silk, hand-beaten gold zari, and pressed archival paper. The emotional atmosphere should evoke stepping into a private salon in Jaipur or Milan: serene, hushed, authoritative, and profoundly tactile.

## Colors

The palette is derived from classic atelier materials—unbleached raw silks, aged gold leaf, dark seasoned teakwood, and oxidized bronze.

- **Primary (`#1A1412`) — Obsidian Espresso:** A dense, rich black-brown providing uncompromising structure, deep ink contrast for typography, and grounding frames.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`) — Royal Champagne Gold:** A luminous, refined metallic tone reserved for badges of craft, primary focal actions, fine borders, and crest accents. Never loud or brassy.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6239`) — Warm Antique Bronze:** A muted, grounding metallic used for secondary links, hover underlines, subtle badges, and bespoke woven tags.
- **Neutral Base (`#F9F6F0`) — Kora Cream:** A soft, tactile alabaster ground that eliminates cold, clinical pure whites in favor of natural textile warmth.

### Functional Tones
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#F3EEE5`):** Warm Sand beige for inset containers, curated swatches, and card backdrops.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#ECE5D8`):** Deep Ecru for dropdowns, luxury drawers, and tactile popovers.
- **Muted Hairline (`rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.08)`):** Whispering borders evoking fine thread counts.
- **Gold Hairline (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`):** Used for heritage dividers and gold leaf containment lines.

## Typography

The type system balances the regal, high-contrast proportion of **Playfair Display** with the clean, modern geometric clarity of **Outfit**.

- **Editorial Headings:** Playfair Display introduces literary authority and couture poise. It is treated with ample leading and generous vertical margins. Italicized cuts may be used selectively for artisan provenance notes, collection subtitles, or saree drape names.
- **Modern Utilitarian Body:** Outfit provides clean readability at small sizes, creating a stark, high-fashion contrast to the serif display. Generous line heights (`1.6` - `1.875`) maintain an airy, unhurried reading pace.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** `label-caps` is always styled in uppercase with dramatic tracking (`0.18em`), reminiscent of embossed luxury retail tags and museum catalog entries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows an **Architectural Modular Grid** with deliberate, generous negative space.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container of `1440px`. The large outer margins (`4rem`) create a curated, gallery-like presentation.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Product cards gracefully compress into alternating asymmetrical rhythm.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column layout with tight `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding to maximize visual asset impact while preserving breathability.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Use large vertical section spacers (`space-xl` scaled by multiples of 2 or 3) to prevent clutter and encourage contemplative browsing.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately minimizes simulated drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering**, **Subtle Textile Overlays**, and **Low-Contrast Filament Borders**.

- **Atmospheric Depth:** Depth is established by placing lighter panels onto richer cream foundations (e.g., `#F3EEE5` on `#F9F6F0`).
- **Filament Borders:** Surfaces are bordered by ultra-fine hairlines (`rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.08)` or champagne metallic `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`).
- **Floating Modals & Drawers:** High-elevation components (cart drawer, bespoke appointment dialogs, drape previewers) employ an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with warm espresso: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.12)`.
- **Textile Grain:** Translucent ambient noise (1-2% opacity) may be applied over solid backgrounds to simulate the natural warmth of unbleached silk and textured weave.

## Shapes

The shape language is strictly **Sharp (0px radius)**, inspired by cut raw silks, precision fabric bolts, bespoke invitation stationery, and the razor-sharp geometry of heritage loom frames.

- Primary interactive elements (buttons, inputs, cards, image frames) use crisp, unrounded 90-degree corners to communicate architectural rigor and timeless luxury.
- Micro-exceptions (such as color swatch circles or artisan profile miniatures) use circular geometry (`border-radius: 50%`), deliberately contrasting the sharp rectangular grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Couture Action:** Solid Obsidian Espresso (`#1A1412`) background with warm cream text (`#F9F6F0`), uppercase `label-caps`, zero border-radius. Hover transitions smoothly over 350ms to Royal Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`) with espresso text.
- **Secondary / Heritage Button:** Transparent background, framed by a 1px Gold border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.6)`), espresso text. On hover, background fills with a delicate gold wash (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`).
- **Text Links & Inquiries:** Understated serif or uppercase sans text accompanied by a fine horizontal underline that expands outward from center upon hover.

### Cards (Lookbook & Saree Showcase)
- Crisp rectangular frames with no drop shadow. Image containers adhere to a classic high-fashion 4:5 or 3:4 aspect ratio.
- Micro-interactions: Upon hover, imagery undergoes a subtle slow zoom (`transform: scale(1.025)` over 700ms ease-out) while metadata reveals craft origin, weave technique (e.g., *Kanjeevaram Silk*, *Banarasi Zari*), and private inquiry links.
- Border accents: Gold hairline divider (`1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`) cleanly separates visual media from description.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Minimalist underline or borderless box style: 1px bottom border (`rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.2)`).
- Active/Focus: Transitions to a solid 1px Royal Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`) line with zero outline glow. Floating label moves upward using `label-caps` in Tertiary Bronze (`#8C6239`).

### Chips & Tags
- Rectangular badge with 1px border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`), cream background, and crisp uppercase typography. Used exclusively for weave authenticity markers (e.g., *HANDLOOM CERTIFIED*, *PURE MULBERRY SILK*).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkboxes: Clean 14px sharp squares. When checked, filled with Obsidian Espresso with an intricate champagne checkmark.
- Radios: 14px sharp outer diamond or round wireframe containing a centered solid brass dot when active.

### Lists & Archival Navigation
- Editorial tabular lists separated by faint hairline dividers (`rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.06)`). Hovering over an item shifts the item slightly to the right (`translateX(6px)`) with secondary detail revealing in antique bronze italics.

### Bespoke Couture Additions
- **Fabric Swatch Palette:** Circular fabric swatches with active selection designated by a double concentric hairline ring in champagne gold.
- **Artisan Weaver Provenance Seal:** A subtle, centered decorative emblem with a thin gold border, highlighting handloom authenticity, weaving village, and master weaver lineage.
