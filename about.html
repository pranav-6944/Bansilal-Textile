The Founding Story

Born from a Vision to Democratize Heritage

In 1994, the Bansilal family established their first small store in the sacred city of Alandi Devachi — a place of pilgrimage and community. The founding vision was simple but bold: bring the finest handloom weaves from master weavers across India directly to the families of Maharashtra, without the unnecessary margin-layers and without compromise on purity.

From a modest beginning of 200 saree varieties, Bansilal Textile has grown to stock over 50,000 unique textile varieties, spanning six distinct collections — and has dressed hundreds of thousands of Maharashtrian families through their most cherished celebrations.

"Every saree we sell carries the fingerprints of a weaver who gave their lifetime mastering this art. We are merely the bridge between their loom and your celebrations."

— The Bansilal Textile Family

Our Philosophy

What We Stand For

verified

Purity First

Every silk piece we sell is tested for real mulberry content. Every zari thread is verified for pure silver or gold content. We never allow adulteration to enter our halls.

handshake

Weaver Welfare

Direct partnerships with over 500 weaver cooperatives ensure artisans receive fair wages. We never exploit traditional knowledge and work to expand the financial dignity of handloom communities.

savings

Honest Value

We believe that heirloom quality should not be the exclusive privilege of the wealthy. Our buying volumes allow us to extend premium quality to middle-class and farming families at accessible prices.
